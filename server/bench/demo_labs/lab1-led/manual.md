# Lab 1 · Light an LED

## Step 1 · Power the rails
Connect the red wire from the **+** rail next to column a to hole **6a**.

## Step 2 · Add the resistor
Put the 220 Ω resistor R1 from **6c** to **10c**.

## Step 3 · Add the LED
Put the LED D1 with the **long leg (anode) in 10d** and the short leg (cathode) in **13d**. Then run the black wire from **13a** to the **−** rail.
