Probe pod wiring (kit breadboard next to the Uno)

Exact holes for firmware/WIRING.md (#5). Tags like A0 mean "jumper from this hole to that Uno pin". Probe wires leave from the j side, toward the dock's Y/B/G/W labels (#37).

Probes: Uno pin → row a · 10 kΩ across the gap · probe wire from row j
Button: diagonal legs 20f (→ D2) and 22e (→ GND), so it works in either rotation
RGB LED: long leg 27b → GND (common-cathode) or 5 V (common-anode); R/G/B → D9/D10/D11 via 220 Ω
